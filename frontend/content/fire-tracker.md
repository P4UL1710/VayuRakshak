# Regional Pollution Signals
The Fire Tracker displays 114 illustrative active regional fire detections in the last 24 hours. The highest cluster is Punjab. Northwesterly wind indicates potential influence on Delhi tomorrow morning, with medium confidence. Seven-day trends and wind ribbons show regional context.

Farm fires may contribute to regional air pollution. They are one of several sources: local traffic, dust, industry, weather and regional transport can combine to worsen Delhi’s air.
