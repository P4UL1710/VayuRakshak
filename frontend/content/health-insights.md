# Your Air Protection Plan
Health Insights offers selectable General, Child, Asthma, Elderly and Fitness preferences. These preferences adapt environmental wellness guidance; they do not diagnose disease. Review the forecast, consider reducing prolonged outdoor exertion during poor air, and choose a lower-exposure commute. For concerning symptoms or urgent needs, consider consulting a clinician.

Optional demo wearable values are sleep score 72, resting heart rate +4 bpm and HRV trend -9%. These are illustrative wellness observations and cannot establish that pollution caused a health change. A shareable local summary can be previewed and downloaded only on request. Disconnecting the wearable hides its trends. No doctor booking or automatic sharing is available.
