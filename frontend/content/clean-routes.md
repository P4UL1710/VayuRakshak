# Clean Routes
The demo corridor runs from IIT Delhi to Connaught Place. Fastest: 42 minutes, exposure score 86. Cleanest: 56 minutes, score 49, 43% lower estimated exposure. Balanced: 48 minutes, score 62, 28% lower estimated exposure. Recommended departure is 8:55 AM via the Balanced Route, saving 8 minutes compared with Cleanest.

Estimated exposure combines route duration, forecast PM2.5, traffic intensity and local air-quality signals. Percentage reduction equals (86 minus alternative score) / 86, rounded. It is not measured inhaled dose or clinical risk. Walking, cycling, two-wheeler, car and metro options use demo duration multipliers. Custom destinations still display the illustrative sample corridor. The 35-minute delay example uses a separate forecast-timing scenario.
