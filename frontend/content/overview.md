# VayuRakshak in one minute
VayuRakshak AI helps Delhi-NCR residents act before poor air quality affects their routine.

First, it tracks air conditions and forecasts when pollution risk may rise. The MVP uses clearly labelled illustrative data, not a live sensor feed.

Second, it compares commute routes and suggests lower-exposure options based on predicted air conditions, travel time, and local signals.

Third, it turns this information into a Tomorrow’s Air Plan—showing when to leave, which route to take, and what indoor protection steps may help.

Finally, if you opt in, Protection Autopilot can schedule a connected purifier when your chosen indoor protection threshold is reached. You always approve automation rules, calendar changes, and data connections.

VayuRakshak is designed as wellness and environmental decision support. It does not provide medical diagnosis. We don’t just inform. We protect.
