# AirProof: safe reports and community evidence
AirProof is VayuRakshak’s second pillar: Fix My Area Tomorrow. Protect yourself today. Fix your air tomorrow. It helps document suspected local pollution concerns safely and organize anonymized community evidence. It does not determine legal responsibility, identify an offender, prove the cause of an AQI spike or provide legal advice.

To report pollution, open AirProof, select what you observed, add a local photo if safe, confirm the area and observation time, review completeness, and edit an English or Hindi complaint draft. Stay away from smoke, fire, traffic, machinery and private property. Do not include names, faces, vehicle plates, phone numbers or home addresses.

The evidence completeness score measures supporting information: media 30 points, location 20, time 15, useful description 10, valid image 10, nearby similar observations 10 and user confirmation 5. The result is capped at 100. It is not proof, visual verification, legal certainty or source attribution. Image brightness is only a simple low-light proxy. No advanced smoke, dust, face or plate model is active.

Location is protected by approximate area cells of roughly 2 km. Public reports withhold exact coordinates, reporter identities, uploaded media, free-text descriptions and edited drafts. The optional full-frame image blur is a local preview, not automatic face or plate detection. Drafts and images stay in memory in this browser session and reset on refresh.

A hotspot means repeated anonymized citizen observations in an approximate area. Pattern confidence reflects report consistency and completeness. It does not prove a source caused pollution or determine responsibility. Reports and regional AQI are separate context.

Community reports help residents and institutions organize neutral observations. Filter the hotspot map, preview an evidence summary and download printable HTML, or use Print / Save as PDF. Create an editable collective complaint for an authority, institution or resident group. Exports are prepared locally.

AirProof will not file a complaint for you. Authority portal buttons are honest placeholders until a verified reporting URL is configured. No automatic submission, official receipt, guaranteed enforcement or measured improvement is claimed. Local duplicate checks, a three-report area/category hourly limit, publishing acknowledgement and correction requests provide prototype safeguards. A production release needs authenticated persistence, real moderation, distributed limits, media privacy review and verified authority integrations.
